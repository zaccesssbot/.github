# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

This is a personal automation account. Interactions here (issues, discussions, comments) should be:

- Respectful and constructive
- On topic and relevant to the repository
- Free of spam, harassment or hate speech

## Enforcement

A violation results in removal and blocking from the repository. To report an issue, contact: contact@isaacadjei.me
